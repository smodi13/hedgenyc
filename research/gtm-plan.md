# 30 / 60 / 90-day NYC pilot, subject to Kalshi policies and authorization

## 0-30: Source and qualify

- Maintain two funnels: primary-source verified and discovery-only. NEVER treat them as equally validated.
- Choose 12 primary-source prospects spanning marine tourism, logistics, gardening, cycling, and walking tours. Exclude businesses already presented as Kalshi hedging examples on https://kalshi.com/hedging from 'new prospect' counts.
- Before each interview assemble: one source-backed fact, plausible risk hypothesis, question about owner contribution margin, possible market, and obvious mismatch.
- Interview framework (no account opening, no trading): What *actual lost cash flow* is material? Which variable causes it? How quickly can you reprice or reschedule? Who holds risk? Is there historical data? Which mitigants do you already use? Are payouts triggered by the same event as your losses?

## 31-60: Test matched economics

- Seek up to five voluntary conversations with business owners, transparently as an independent applicant doing research, and only with permission to document anonymized answers. No interviews have occurred as of the report date.
- Collect business-provided weekly volumes only with explicit consent. Otherwise remain hypothetical and prominent in all charts.
- Backtest station data against documented cancellation conditions if available. Produce a basis error matrix, false-positive settlement cases and false-negative loss cases.
- Validate available contracts, limit-order buy quantity, spread, contract expiry, maximum possible premium + fees, and whether the desired number of contracts can actually be filled near expected price. Do not imply book depth equals guaranteed fill.

## 61-90: Create decision-ready pilot briefs

- Recommend pilot only when: owner verifies exposure; contract resolution matches loss timing/location; premium and basis risk are understood; liquidity supports intended size; business can afford to lose entire premium; and onboarding/compliance authorization exists.
- Escalate unsuitable listed contracts to Markets team *as feedback*, never invent a market and label it live.
- Publish anonymized learnings where permission exists: reasons to pass are as informative as conversion.

## Recommended operational metrics

- Qualified prospect count: primary-source verified businesses where business risk trigger has been identified, not directory matches.
- Owner conversations: count only real confirmed conversations.
- Trigger-match ratio: proportion of documented loss events matching settlement conditions, conditional on measured data and sample sizes.
- Projected premium-to-loss ratio: premiums plus fees relative to owner-verified loss distribution, **not gross revenue**.
- Liquidity-to-need ratio: available firm near-touch size versus requested notional, with time stamps and slippage caveats.
- Product feedback: documented market gaps and repeatable hypotheses, not speculative total addressable market numbers.

## Prototype pipeline stages

Research discovered -> Company source verified -> Exposure hypothesis -> Owner consented interview -> Financial loss quantified -> Contract/rule matched -> Price & liquidity vetted -> Compliance/partner review -> Not suitable / Potential pilot.

*At launch, every record is NOT CONTACTED. No responses or pilots are fabricated.*
