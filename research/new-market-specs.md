# Market-creation opportunities | Hypotheses, not proposed Kalshi listings

These are independent product research opportunities, not currently promised, approved, or necessarily permissible contracts. The Kalshi Markets team would need to evaluate regulatory permissibility, manipulability, resolution independence, market-maker support, customer demand, and liquidity economics.

## Spec A | NYC weekend sustained high-wind / gust event

**Job to be done**: wind-triggered safety disruption for harbor charters, outdoor construction, and some visitor operations. **Candidate question**: Will the official observation at a specified station exceed a predefined sustained-wind or gust threshold during a defined NYC Saturday-Sunday window? **Settlement**: specified station, data publisher, units, sample frequency, handling missing observations, revision cutoff and exact interval all must be established before listing. **Hedging limitation**: Central Park and airport wind can materially differ from harbor wind; cancellations could occur below or above threshold. **Demand test**: obtain five nonbinding owner explanations of cancellation policy and threshold before seeking product development. **No contract ticker invented.**

## Spec B | NYC extreme rain during operating hours

**Job to be done**: businesses harmed by heavy single-day rainfall, where any-measurable-rain markets trigger too easily. **Candidate question**: Does official NYC Central Park station record >= 1.0 inch precipitation in a clearly defined 24-hour window? **Settlement**: NWS / observation source, precise site, raw vs corrected reading, day boundary and revisions. **Limitation**: borough-by-borough flooding and business cancellations may vary materially from Central Park. **Priority**: medium, pending historical correlation tests.

## Spec C | East Coast or NYC diesel fuel cost index

**Job to be done**: delivery companies whose local gallons and fixed contract prices are exposed to diesel hikes. **Candidate question**: Is a specified authoritative EIA Central Atlantic or NYC retail diesel index above a threshold on publication date? **Settlement**: official named series, exact publication time, revision policy, strike, strike unit, holiday handling and missing publication treatment. **Limitation**: a threshold pays a fixed $1 per contract and still will not replicate per-gallon expense; weekly settlements versus long-term purchase costs differ. **Priority**: investigate after verified buyer interest and market feasibility.

## Falsification and prioritization

Reject concepts if: (1) business loss trigger cannot be documented; (2) underlying observable index cannot be resolved unambiguously; (3) mismatch remains economically material; (4) desired exposures are too infrequent/fragmented for sustainable two-sided liquidity; (5) a cheaper physical mitigation, surcharge, insurance, or existing future offers better protection. Neither a theoretically useful hedge nor a customer interview guarantees product fit.
