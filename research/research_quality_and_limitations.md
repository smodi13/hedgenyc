# Source integrity and current scope | HedgeNYC

**Prepared:** November 6, 2026. **Business origination universe:** 47 distinct named businesses.

## Research quality tiers

- **33 primary company-website-backed records:** first-party pages support existence and the specific stated business activity, published policies or advertised product details. They do **not** establish owner financial exposure, customer status, forecasted revenue, profit, trading willingness, or hedge suitability. Some records cite general company service offerings rather than exact weather cancellation policies.
- **14 discovery-only records:** surfaced by local business directories and/or discovery searches. They do **not** have a verified first-party source in the dataset. These should remain explicitly lower confidence and should NOT be counted as ready-to-contact or qualified hedging customers.
- **3 flagship cases** (Brooklyn Sail, Xentra Transport, Arborpolitan): public operating evidence plus plainly hypothetical margin assumptions, with cautionary analyst decisions. These are not performed due diligence or owner consultations.
- **5 discovered public Kalshi market / family records:** original URLs included but no live quote, current order book, execution capacity or active market availability validated. Market examples are dated.

The `prospects.json` field `analyst_screen_score` is a subjective triage output from stated 30/20/30/20 weighted factors, not a statistical model or verified business performance. The accompanying Excel business screen calculates score through formulas, with weight cells editable.

## Decision discipline

1. Distinguish sourced operating detail vs inferred business exposure vs hypothetical calculations vs executable market data.
2. Review settlement rule at the exact contract level before discussing protection.
3. Value a loss at contribution margin or genuine incremental cash cost, not necessarily gross ticket value.
4. Include the loss-without-payout state, not only payout-wins states.
5. Treat a lack of suitable contract, basis match or affordable liquidity as a reason to pass.
6. Do not imply either positive hedge returns or that these businesses have engaged with Kalshi.
7. Avoid scraping personal contact information or making unsolicited communication as if employed by Kalshi.

## Source fidelity

First-party business/source URLs appear in every relevant prospect record and in `sources.csv` for central claims. The `case-studies.md` notes further source and assumption provenance. Kalshi general taker fee documentation: https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf (official published PDF viewed in November 2026). Be alert to market-specific schedule differences, revisions, fills and different rounding rules.

The independent NYC rain information is grounded in https://www.nyc.gov/content/climate/pages/extreme-rainfall and public city weather event summaries. Climate exposure context does not imply every named business lost money in a weather event.

## What would be needed for a genuine pilot

- Permissioned owner interviews and a record of whether businesses agree to be discussed.
- Historical owner-level volumes, booking cancellations, lost-margin and fuel-expense data.
- Exact Kalshi contract rulebook for matching station, index, strike, period, time and settlement contingencies.
- Time-stamped executable asks / order-book depth after fees/slippage, updated before any proposal.
- Internal risk, business development and compliance review of educational materials and any eventual customer engagement.

The current handoff intentionally does not claim these are completed. Its purpose is to demonstrate research rigor, suitability judgment and ability to build a repeatable tool.
