# HedgeNYC | Independent NYC Hedging Origination and Research Lab

**Date:** November 6, 2026  
**Status:** Research-supported engineering handoff, not a deployed product  
**Independence:** Independently created work sample. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or commissioned by Kalshi.  
**Role focus:** Hedging, Policy & Business Development, New York.

## What is complete

- **47 uniquely named NYC business records**, of which **33 have directly cited company websites** and **14 are preliminary directory discoveries** pending independent primary-source diligence. Do not falsely display all 47 as vetted.
- Three research-backed flagship case studies, including deliberately unfavorable market-fit conclusions.
- Five Kalshi market / market-family discovery records with direct official links, settlement mismatches, and explicit non-live status.
- Source register, evidentiary constraints, market-development briefs, outreach plan, honest site copy, detailed art direction, formula-driven financial model, and implementation/test contract.

## Research takeaways

1. Sailing businesses may lose bookings in unsafe weather, but an any-measurable-rain market is not a good physical-loss trigger. A strong sales analyst must sometimes **decline the hedge**.
2. Xentra advertises no fuel surcharge, making fuel inflation worth asking about, but mixed gasoline/diesel fleets and national-vs-local indices may prevent useful coverage.
3. Arborpolitan has offsetting downside and upside under storms: disrupted routine work plus potential emergency demand. Modeling gross cancellations alone could be misleading.
4. Foods of NY Tours and Like A Local publicly say tours generally run rain or shine. These make valuable **negative controls** for simplistic weather prospecting.
5. There are no verified business owner interviews, live market executions, or permission to trade in this package.

## The engineering responsibilities

**ChatGPT owns:** prospect research, factual evidence, case judgments, assumptions, financial methodology, copy, scoring, product policy, and go-to-market recommendation. Treat these files as authoritative editorial input.  
**Claude Code owns only:** coding, architecture, animations, smooth scrolling, design implementation, unit tests, accessibility, API integration, build, Vercel deployment, visual regression, and engineering documentation. Do NOT ask Claude to research new businesses, invent financial assumptions, rewrite underwriting conclusions, or decide that a hedge is appropriate. When data is missing, expose the evidence gap instead of improvising a fact.

## Public-source cautions

- A website assertion is an observed public claim, not audited fact; owner economics are unknown.
- A named business in the screen is not necessarily a Kalshi customer, non-customer, or outreach lead already qualified.
- Market existence and quoted/offer liquidity change. No order book has been verified as executable.
- Kalshi prices reflect tradable contract costs, not objective probability of a business loss.
- Formula-based outcomes and numerical case inputs are hypothetical and not client-specific advice.
- No live outreach or trading is authorized; a researcher should transparently state independent status if contacting anyone.
