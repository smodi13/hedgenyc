## Thesis chapter

H2: `Hedging starts with the business, not the contract.`

Narrative: `A sailor cancels for wind. A price screen pays for rain. Those two events can overlap without being the same risk. Our job is to find the cash-flow exposure, identify the actual settlement trigger, and decide whether the match is strong enough to matter.`

Three rails: `EXPOSURE: What genuinely changes contribution margin?` / `TRIGGER: What does the contract actually settle on?` / `EXECUTION: What does protection cost, and can the business obtain it?`

## Hedge Lab chapter

H2: `See what your hedge covers. See what it doesn't.`

Subhead: `Run four states, including the one most sales pitches leave out: a real operating loss when the financial contract does not pay.`

Metrics: `Premium + general fee`, `Payoff if contract settles YES`, `Business margin loss`, `Expected result under illustrative joint states`, `False positive/negative basis mismatch`.

Sensitivity controls: loss amount, contract count, YES limit price, generic taker multiplier, four joint scenario weights. Label all defaults `ILLUSTRATIVE`, never `LIVE QUOTE` unless verified API response with timestamp. Show `price is not a calibrated business loss probability`.
